Deep Learning & Statistical Foundation

Engineered for empirical continuous shelf-life regression rather than superficial discrete classification.

🧠

Transfer Learning Regressor

Pretrained ImageNet backbones (ResNet50 / EfficientNet-B0) extract dense color histograms, texture gradients, and necrotic surface spots. Replaced classification heads with a continuous linear head and ReLU output to prevent impossible negative days.

📊

Conformal Uncertainty Bounds

Single-point predictions are inherently stochastic. We use distribution-free Inductive Conformal Prediction calibrated on held-out samples. The non-conformity residual quantile guarantees rigorous 90% finite-sample coverage without fabricated intervals.

🛡️

Zero-Leakage Group Splitting

Time-series photographs of the same physical specimen (e.g., BANANA_001 on Day 0, 1, 2...) are strictly held within the same split using GroupShuffleSplit. This prevents data leakage and eliminates artificially inflated evaluation metrics.

Standardized Spoilage Endpoints

Empirical criteria defining the exact termination threshold for each perishable category.

🍌

Banana (Nominal: 10d)

Endpoint: Stage 7+ senescent collapse. Over 70% dark brown surface necrosis, pulp liquefaction, or microbial colonization at stem, rendering the fruit unpalatable.

🥑

Avocado (Nominal: 8d)

Endpoint: Internal vascular browning and skin indentation collapse. Severe rancid oxidation, rancidity odor, stringy dark flesh, or mycelium emergence.

🍞

Bread (Nominal: 6d)

Endpoint: Macroscopic fungal sporulation. First visible pinpoint colonies of Rhizopus stolonifer or Penicillium, or total starch retrogradation and unyielding crust petrification.
