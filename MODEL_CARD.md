# MODEL CARD: DAYS TO DECAY — Perishable Aging Regressor

## 1. Model Details

- **Model Name:** Days to Decay Shelf-Life Regressor
- **Architecture:** Pretrained ResNet50 (or EfficientNet-B0) with Custom Continuous Regression Head
- **Head Architecture:** AdaptiveAvgPool2d -> Flatten -> Linear(2048, 256) -> BatchNorm1d -> LeakyReLU(0.1) -> Dropout(0.3) -> Linear(256, 1) -> ReLU
- **Framework:** PyTorch 2.x, Torchvision
- **Input Dimensions:** 3 x 224 x 224 RGB image (ImageNet normalized)
- **Output:** Continuous scalar representing estimated remaining shelf life in natural days ($\hat{y} \in [0.0, \infty)$)
- **Uncertainty Method:** Distribution-free Inductive Conformal Prediction ($1 - \alpha = 0.90$)

---

## 2. Intended Use

- **Primary Intended Use:** Estimation of remaining useful shelf life from smartphone or webcam photographs of perishable goods (banana, avocado, bread) stored under standard ambient room temperature conditions.
- **Intended Users:** Food inventory managers, grocery consumers, waste-reduction researchers, and automated pantry organizers.

---

## 3. Out-of-Scope and Prohibited Uses

- **Food Microbiology & Pathogen Detection:** Visual appearance does not correlate directly with invisible bacterial contamination (e.g., *Salmonella enterica*, *Listeria monocytogenes*, *Escherichia coli*). The model must never be used to certify microbiological safety.
- **Commercial Food Safety Certification:** The model is an experimental visual aging estimator, not a certified regulatory food inspector.
- **Unsupported Food Categories:** Predictions on unrepresented food categories (e.g., meats, dairy, leafy greens) are unsupported and scientifically invalid.

---

## 4. Uncertainty Estimation: Conformal Prediction

Unlike naive point estimates that falsely project certainty, the model implements **Inductive Conformal Prediction**:

1. A calibration set $\mathcal{D}_{\text{cal}} = \{(x_i, y_i)\}_{i=1}^n$ held out from training is evaluated.
2. Absolute non-conformity residuals $s_i = |y_i - \hat{y}(x_i)|$ are computed.
3. For a target confidence level $1 - \alpha = 0.90$, the conformal quantile $\hat{q}$ is chosen:
   $$\hat{q} = \text{Quantile}\left(s_1, \dots, s_n; \frac{\lceil (n+1)(1-\alpha) \rceil}{n}\right)$$
4. For any new test image $x_{\text{new}}$, the prediction interval is:
   $$C(x_{\text{new}}) = \left[ \max(0.0, \hat{y}(x_{\text{new}}) - \hat{q}),\ \hat{y}(x_{\text{new}}) + \hat{q} \right]$$

This interval guarantees finite-sample coverage $P(y \in C(x)) \ge 1 - \alpha$ under the empirical training conditions. If the calibration set is unpopulated, the API explicitly returns `"uncertainty_method": "unavailable"` rather than fabricating false confidence bounds.

---

## 5. Evaluation Methodology

The model is evaluated on held-out test splits with zero `sample_id` leakage using continuous regression metrics:
- **MAE (Mean Absolute Error in days):** Average absolute deviation from ground truth.
- **RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error in days):** Penalizes larger deviation outliers.
- **$R^2$ Score:** Proportion of variance explained by visual degradation features.
- **Median Absolute Error (MedAE):** Robust 50th percentile error.
- **Tolerance Bounds:** Percentage of predictions within $\pm 0.5$ days and $\pm 1.0$ days of ground truth.
- **Baseline Benchmark:** Benchmarked against a naive model predicting the training-set mean remaining shelf life.

---

## 6. Known Limitations

1. **Storage Condition Sensitivity:** Visual aging accelerates significantly under elevated temperature and humidity. An avocado at 30°C decays faster than one at 18°C despite similar initial appearance.
2. **Hidden Internal Defects:** Internal rot or stone mold in avocados may not exhibit external skin cues until advanced senescence.
3. **Lighting Variations:** Extreme specular glare or warm incandescent lighting can mimic yellow/brown aging hues.

---

## 7. Food Safety Disclaimer

> [!CAUTION]
> **FOOD SAFETY DISCLAIMER:**
> Predictions generated by DAYS TO DECAY are empirical estimates of visual condition and do not establish microbiological food safety. Discard any perishable food that exhibits signs of mold, unpleasant odors, slime, structural collapse, or discoloration, regardless of the predicted remaining days.
